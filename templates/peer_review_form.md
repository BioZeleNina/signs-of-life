# Peer review form
## Signs of life -- Mission log and mission report review

*Complete this form for the mission log and mission report you have
been assigned to review. Aim for 400--600 words total. Be specific.
Be constructive.*

**Reviewer:** [your name]
**Work reviewed:** [author name]

## Part A -- Mission log review

The mission log is graded on reproducibility and computational
hygiene. Could someone else re-run the analysis from these notes?

**A1 -- Reproducibility**

*Could you reproduce the key analysis steps from the log alone --
without the report or prior knowledge of the methods? Note which
steps are well-documented and which are missing or unclear.*

**A2 -- Computational hygiene**

*Are commands recorded with their parameters? Are decisions
explained (e.g. why a particular threshold was chosen)? Are
outputs noted? Give one example of good practice and one suggestion
for improvement.*

**A3 -- Completeness**

*Does the log appear to cover all eight sessions? Are there any
obvious gaps between sessions or within a session's steps?*

### Section 3 -- One area for improvement

*Identify one claim where you think the evidence is weaker than
presented, or where the interpretation could be more carefully
qualified. Be specific about which claim, which evidence, and why
you think the connection is not fully established.*

This is not a criticism of the author. Good science requires
scepticism even about your own results.

Examples of useful feedback:
- "The conclusion that X is true is stated with more certainty than
  the p-value justifies. A p-value of 0.04 with n=3 is consistent
  with the result but not conclusive."
- "The report states that Y explains the previous surveys missing
  the organism, but does not rule out the alternative explanation
  that Z."

---

### Section 4 -- One question

*Ask one scientific question you would want answered if you were
an editor reviewing this as a journal submission. It could be a
question about missing data, an alternative interpretation, or a
follow-up experiment.*

---

### Section 5 -- Overall assessment

*One sentence: Is this report ready to transmit to Earth Command
as written, or does it need revision first? What would you
recommend?*

---

*Reviewing other people's work is a scientific skill. It requires
reading carefully, thinking critically, and communicating respectfully.
The goal is to make the work better, not to win an argument.*
