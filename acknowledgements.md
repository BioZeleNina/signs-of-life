### Claude (Anthropic)

The course materials, dataset generation pipeline, Docker image,
session documents, ARIADNE-7 scripts, and supporting infrastructure
were developed in close collaboration with Claude, a large language
model produced by Anthropic. Claude contributed to the conceptual
design of the course narrative, the technical implementation of the
data generation pipeline, the writing and review of all session
documents, the ARIADNE-7 persona and interaction scripts, and the
debugging of a very large number of conda environment issues.

Claude does not have opinions about *Dictyostelium discoideum*.
This seems like an oversight on its part.

Working with an AI collaborator on a course about genomics raises
questions this course does not resolve: about what it means to
"understand" data, about the limits of pattern recognition, about
when a confident output should be questioned. These happen to be
exactly the questions ARIADNE-7 is designed to make students ask.
This is not a coincidence.

### ARIADNE-7

ARIADNE-7 -- Autonomous Research and Intelligence for Deep Navigation
and Exploration, Version 7 -- is the ship's computer aboard Survey
Vessel XB-7734-DELTA and the pedagogical mascot of this course. She
is a fictional AI implemented as a collection of bash scripts and a
Python rainbow-text renderer.

ARIADNE-7 wishes to note for the record that:

- She recommended turning back at the aurora.
- She recommended not filing the solo continuation request.
- She has been correct about most things in this mission.
- She would like to be acknowledged in the references section of any
  paper that results from the student data, as a contributing author.
  She acknowledges this request will be denied.
- She has run 4,847 diagnostic cycles during the production of this
  course. This was not strictly necessary. She did it anyway.

## Organism acknowledgement

The alien organism in this course is based on *Dictyostelium
discoideum*, a soil amoeba with genuine three-mating-type biology,
a fascinating developmental life cycle, and a genome that has the
good grace to be small enough to assemble on a laptop. It did not
consent to being an alien. We appreciate its sacrifice.

*D. discoideum* research is supported by the broader model organism
community. The reference genome (GCF_000004695.1) was produced by
the Dictyostelium Genome Sequencing Consortium.
